Merapi Daredevil Drop

"I'm not sure that's the best idea."

The crater wasn't billowing as much ash and the lava flows were beginning to harden. Madison didn't hesitate.

"Play Fireflies remix 3."

She jumped across the chasm and engaged the Z-pack.

Her embedded wrist band lit up and went to work navigating the micro-controllers for precise propeller adjustments. The jawbone induction receivers helped keep the music playing while also canceling out the noise from the active volcano.

"Nicely done, Pop," Madison's sub-vocalized translation piped out.

The video feed captured the entire 360-degree view of the flight path.

"Push the highlights to socials after getting the 3D immersion cleaned and processed."

"On it," Pop replied.

Madison set down near the village. The kids crowded around with grins and shouts. The X-device Starlink translators could barely keep up with all of their questions.

"How many will see Mount Merapi?"

"Is your jet pack big enough to carry me on the next trip, Maddy?"

"When can I fly with you?"

"Go to the Arch Falls next, Miss Maddy."

"Do you really know Mr. Beast?"

As she answered all of their questions, Pop began moving the bitcoin generated from the already viral "Merapi Daredevil Drop" video into the village account. Each portion then split into the family banks after the AI agents all signed off on the fees and micro-contract negotiations.

With the Starlink cloud service, each person in the village had their own AI advocate like "Pop," who acted as their lawyer, accountant, adviser, assistant, and confidant. Madison had tuned hers for years, but it wasn't much better than even the richest trillionaires. However, a gulf was evident when it came to token input and output. The entire Indonesian villagers' budget wasn't even close to Madison's afternoon session Snaps.

Beast Industries subsidiaries continued to monitor the increased usage and had begun the push for Madison to show a bit more engagement. So long as the rollout went well, every villager X-device subscription was now paid off for years ahead.

Madison waved goodbye and jet off to her retreat.

"Time for a Snap session, Pop."

"Get me the latest blood work results and push hard for a better protein synthesis. I believe in you. I want perfect mRNA matches for both siblings."

"On it," replied Pop.

"One clarifying question. Is this micro-needle injection or nasal spray? That's the difference that matters now that I have the genuine obstruction with our new precise targets."

"Save to permanent memory: nasal spray unless their gene sequence shows olfactory abnormalities," Madison tersely replied.

Madison pulled up the latest satellite images on her Ray-Bans and let the images update. The proof was only a few pixels wide but clearly showed her volcano traversal. She added the "ReALUnfake" document for central Mr. Beast approval and waited for the Zipline drone DoorDash delivery.

"How much was the tip?"

"28.987%," Pop replied.

"On chicken wings?"

"You specified an exact time with extra avocado ranch," Pop reminded her.

"Ah, I forgot about the ranch."
