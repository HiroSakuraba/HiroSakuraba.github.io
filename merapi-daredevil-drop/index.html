Merapi Daredevil Drop

"I'm not sure that's the best idea."

The crater wasn't billowing as much ash and the lava flows were beginning to harden. Madison didn't hesitate.

"Play Fireflies remix 3."

She jumped across the chasm and engaged the Z-pack.

Her embedded wrist band lit up and went to work navigating the micro-controllers for precise propeller adjustments. The jawbone induction receivers helped keep the music playing while also canceling out the noise from the active volcano.

"Nicely done, Pop," Madison's sub-vocalized translation piped out.

The video feed captured the entire 360-degree view of the flight path.

"Push the highlights to socials after getting the 3D immersion cleaned and processed."

"On it," Pop replied.

Madison set down near the village. The kids crowded around with grins and shouts. The X-device Starlink translators could barely keep up with all of their questions.

"How many will see Mount Merapi?"

"Is your jet pack big enough to carry me on the next trip, Maddy?"

"When can I fly with you?"

"Go to the Arch Falls next, Miss Maddy."

"Do you really know Mr. Beast?"

As she answered all of their questions, Pop began moving the bitcoin generated from the already viral "Merapi Daredevil Drop" video into the village account. Each portion then split into the family banks after the AI agents all signed off on the fees and micro-contract negotiations.

With the Starlink cloud service, each person in the village had their own AI advocate like "Pop," who acted as their lawyer, accountant, adviser, assistant, and confidant. Madison had tuned hers for years, but it wasn't much better than even the richest trillionaires. However, a gulf was evident when it came to token input and output. The entire Indonesian villagers' budget wasn't even close to Madison's afternoon session Snaps.

Beast Industries subsidiaries continued to monitor the increased usage and had begun the push for Madison to show a bit more engagement. So long as the rollout went well, every villager X-device subscription was now paid off for years ahead.

Madison waved goodbye and jet off to her retreat.

"Time for a Snap session, Pop."

"Get me the latest blood work results and push hard for a better protein synthesis. I believe in you. I want perfect mRNA matches for both siblings."

"On it," replied Pop.

"One clarifying question. Is this micro-needle injection or nasal spray? That's the difference that matters now that I have the genuine obstruction with our new precise targets."

"Save to permanent memory: nasal spray unless their gene sequence shows olfactory abnormalities," Madison tersely replied.

Madison pulled up the latest satellite images on her Ray-Bans and let the images update. The proof was only a few pixels wide but clearly showed her volcano traversal. She added the "ReALUnfake" document for central Mr. Beast approval and waited for the Zipline drone DoorDash delivery.

"How much was the tip?"

"28.987%," Pop replied.

"On chicken wings?"

"You specified an exact time with extra avocado ranch," Pop reminded her.

"Ah, I forgot about the ranch."

Kenya

Otieno was on the third reprint when the wrist band pulsed.

"Bore is still 0.14 millimeters tight. Shrinkage doesn't match the last run," Tony chimed in.

"I know."

The part sat in the tray, titanium-gray, almost right. The kite-motor frame on the bench had an empty socket where it was supposed to go. Outside the workshop slats, the Nairobi estate was already loud. An air-trike zipped past the gate. Someone was burning waste two plots over.

"Re-slice with the new curve?" Tony asked.

Otieno thought for a moment. 400 million-ish tokens on the household pool. The aluminum model would take about 90k for one pass. A local AI model would cut cost estimates.

"Local. No cloud," Otieno said.

"Confidence drops," Tony replied.

"Fine."

The enclosure fans wound down. The house tried to preheat the kitchen and he shut that off.

His wife Chioma was calling.

"Hey, we have an issue. The kids were at the Jeongs' for the splash play date."

"Kas dumped the entire polymer sand truck in the koi pond. Xaden used the hose and ruined the pH levels. The big one died and Mrs. Jeong said it was over 60 years old."

Otieno swore under his breath.

The part was still cooling. Opening the chamber early would warp it.

"I'm coming."

"Bring the shop vac. Jenny Jeong already asked if Tony can escrow the cleaning."

"How much?"

Chioma didn't look at him on the video call.

"More than the kite motor," Chioma said in a fake perky tone.

The local AI shop robotic voice cut in.

"Local solve done. Gap 0.09. Still out of spec. Cloud refine is 88k tokens and eighteen minutes."

Eighteen minutes was the walk across the estate if he didn't stop for the vac.

"Save it. Don't spend the cloud."

"Household pool hits empty in six days at the school-assistant rate," Tony replied.

"I said save it."

He left the frame on the bench.

On the path Chioma said, "Xaden asked if Miss Maddy was coming to the estate festival. He saw the volcano drop post on the shared wall. Kas wants an autograph also."

"Tell them no. Let Jenny know we're on the way. Don't let Tony or Ashley sign anything until I see the pond."

"Ashley isn't a pushover like Tony," Chioma bit back.

The 3D printer chamber suddenly dinged and unsealed. He took it out and tried to attach it. The part still didn't fit snugly, but he could probably get it polished enough that it would work.

Otieno's smartglasses marked the Jeongs' courtyard pond yellow.

"Hey Stark, write me a good apology letter, and look up the current exchange rate for an older koi with better color than average."

"Greetings Mr. and Mrs. Yoruba. A stay is filed on both of your behalf. The honorable French Council has overstepped their bounds and decided to execute on our ledger and also felt like skipping the second-booking protocol. Normally, we wouldn't even bother with a face-to-face human call, but we think this is a solid case to defend for future legal inroads against our personage in other jurisdiction cases."

We're still working through the details of the time frame allowed. Please don't talk on the record until Beast Industries can work through future probabilities where we can get a win-win decision in our favor for this unfortunate situation." X Counsel let the emphasis on win-win linger.

Chioma hesitated, decided to quietly rethink her answer, and then said, "You put the wrong name on the pool caption."

"It's what's on the account," X Counsel replied.

"Then change it," Ashley interrupted. "If the estate wall gets it wrong, his job packet gets it wrong."

X Counsel dryly replied, "Caption's changed. No koi bid. Query is valuation for neighbor restitution. Ashley on the household. Tony on the shop."
